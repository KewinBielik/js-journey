Dr. Helen Marlow

Ear, nose and throat specialist. Audiology and voice.

I am an ear, nose and throat doctor in Bristol. I see adults and children for hearing problems, voice problems, sinus trouble, and throat infections. In a visit I listen first, examine carefully, and explain what I find in plain words, including what to do next. My aim is to treat the problem and leave you clear about it.

WHAT WE OFFER

Advanced Care Worth Traveling For:

At The Marlow Clinic I see adults and children for problems of the ear, nose, throat, and voice. A visit starts with a careful history and an examination, and you leave knowing what I found and what happens next. The cards below are the problems people come with most often: hearing and voice, ear infections and wax, sinus trouble, throat and tonsils, children's ear, nose and throat care, and dizziness. If you are not sure which one fits, book a consultation and we will start there.

  • Hearing and voice

    A voice that tires, or hearing that has slowly dropped, is easy to put off. I test both, show you what the results mean, and tell you what can be treated and what to watch.

  • Ear infections and wax

    Pain, pressure, or a blocked ear can make one side feel deaf overnight. I look for infection or wax, clear what is safe to clear in the clinic, and send you home knowing how to keep it from coming straight back.

  • Sinus and nose

    A blocked nose, face pain, or infections that return every winter need more than another short course of spray. I find out whether the problem is swelling, infection, or the shape of the nose, and we agree a plan that lasts longer than the next cold.

  • Throat and tonsils

    A throat that stays sore, tonsils that keep swelling, or food that is hard to swallow should be looked at properly. I examine the throat, explain whether this is infection, enlargement, or something else, and what the next step is.

  • Children's ear, nose and throat

    Children get repeated ear infections, snoring, and sore throats, and the visit itself can be the frightening part. I show them the light first, examine only as far as they can manage, and leave you with a clear plan rather than another unanswered appointment.

  • Dizziness

    Unsteadiness that comes in spells, or a room that spins when you turn your head, often starts in the ear. I sort out whether that is the cause, and you leave knowing what will help and what will not.

EXPERIENCE

My Training And Qualifications:

  • Endoscopic sinus surgery, Edinburgh, 2019.
  • Voice disorders, Manchester, 2021.

I am a member of ENT UK and the British Voice Association. I go to their meetings to keep up with changes in ear surgery and voice care.
