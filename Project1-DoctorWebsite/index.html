Dr. Helen Marlow

Ear, nose and throat specialist. Audiology and voice.

I am an ear, nose and throat doctor in Bristol. I see adults and children for hearing problems, voice problems, sinus trouble, and throat infections. In a visit I listen first, examine carefully, and explain what I find in plain words, including what to do next. My aim is to treat the problem and leave you clear about it.

WHAT WE OFFER

Advanced Care Worth Traveling For:

  • Hearing and voice

    I check how you hear and how your voice sounds, then explain what I find.

  • Ear infections and wax

    I treat painful or blocked ears, including wax that is making it hard to hear.

  • Sinus and nose

    I look after a blocked nose, sinus pain, and infections that keep coming back.

  • Throat and tonsils

    I see sore throats, swollen tonsils, and trouble swallowing.

  • Children's ear, nose and throat

    I see children for ear infections, hearing worries, and throat problems.

  • Dizziness

    I work out whether unsteadiness is coming from the ear, and what to do next.

I see adults and children for problems of the ear, nose, throat, and voice. If you are not sure which one fits, book a consultation and I will explain what I find.
